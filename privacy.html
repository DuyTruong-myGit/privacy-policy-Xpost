1 Who We Are

XPost is a social media management platform that enables users to connect their social media accounts (including TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, and others) and manage content publishing, scheduling, and analytics from a single dashboard.

We act as a data processor on behalf of our users and as a data controller for information we collect directly for operating our platform.

Contact: Xpost.help@gmail.com

2 Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration information (name, email address, password)
  • Profile information you choose to add
  • Content you create and schedule through our platform (posts, videos, captions, hashtags)
  • Communications with our support team

2.2 Information from Social Media Platforms

When you connect your social media accounts (e.g., TikTok, Facebook, Instagram) to XPost, we receive information authorized by you through each platform's OAuth process, which may include:

  • Your public profile information (username, profile picture, bio)
  • Access tokens required to publish content on your behalf
  • Account statistics and analytics data (followers, post reach, engagement)
  • Content you authorize us to read or manage

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time and date)
  • Device information (device type, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (features used, actions taken within the platform)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 9)

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, operate, and maintain our platform, including publishing content to your connected social media accounts.
  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, authenticate you, and ensure platform security.
  • Analytics & Insights: To display performance analytics for your social media accounts within your dashboard.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and resolve technical issues.
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our features and user experience.
  • Security: To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.
  • Communications: To send you service-related notifications and updates (not marketing without consent).
Important: We do NOT use your social media content or data to train machine learning models, sell advertising, or share with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

4 TikTok Data Usage

XPost integrates with the TikTok for Developers API. When you connect your TikTok account, we access only the permissions you explicitly grant. Below is how we handle TikTok-specific data:

4.1 Permissions We Request

  • user.info.basic — To display your TikTok username and profile picture within the XPost dashboard.
  • video.publish — To publish video content to your TikTok account on your behalf.
  • video.upload — To upload video files to TikTok for scheduled or immediate publishing.

4.2 How We Use TikTok Data

  • TikTok access tokens are stored securely and used solely to authenticate API requests made on your behalf.
  • Your TikTok profile information is displayed only within your own XPost dashboard.
  • Video content you schedule or publish through XPost is transmitted to TikTok per your explicit instructions and is not stored on our servers beyond what is necessary for the operation.
  • We do not access, collect, or store TikTok user data beyond what is necessary for the features you use.

4.3 Data Deletion

When you disconnect your TikTok account from XPost or delete your XPost account, we immediately revoke access and delete all associated TikTok access tokens and cached profile data from our systems.

XPost's use of TikTok data is governed by TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Data Compliance Policy.

5 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 With Social Media Platforms

When you publish content through XPost, the content and necessary authentication data are shared with the respective social media platform (e.g., TikTok, Facebook) to complete the action you requested.

5.2 With Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, database services). These providers are contractually bound to use your data only for the purpose of providing services to us and must adhere to our data protection standards.

5.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, government requests), or to protect the rights, property, or safety of XPost, our users, or the public.

5.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice before your personal information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6 Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest (e.g., access tokens)
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Strict access controls — only authorized personnel may access user data
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access

Despite our best efforts, no security system is impenetrable. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

7 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Social media access tokens: Retained only while your account is connected. Deleted immediately upon disconnection.
  • Content data: Retained for the duration of your account. Post history may be retained for up to 12 months after account deletion for legal compliance purposes.
  • Log data: Retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes.

8 Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request that we transfer your data to another service provider in a machine-readable format.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data under certain circumstances.
  • Objection: Object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at Xpost.help@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days. You may also disconnect connected social media accounts at any time through the XPost dashboard, which will immediately revoke our access.

9 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

XPost uses cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our platform. Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function correctly (e.g., session management, authentication).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences to improve your experience.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform (e.g., page views, feature usage) to improve our service.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.
